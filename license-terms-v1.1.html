Short version

Aerini is free and open source under AGPL-3.0. If you'd rather not comply with AGPL-3.0's source-sharing requirement — for example, you're shipping a modified build closed-source, or running a modified aerini-server as part of your own product — you can instead take this commercial license. It's a subscription, billed monthly or yearly, that covers one named product or service and every version of Aerini released while you're subscribed. If you stop paying, you keep the right to use every version you were covered for, and once you've paid for twelve months in total you also keep the right to ship and host those versions in your product. There's no seat count and no phone-home check. Your license certificate — your license key plus the details you enter at checkout — is issued automatically when you pay.
